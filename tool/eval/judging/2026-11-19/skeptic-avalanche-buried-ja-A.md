## Question avalanche-buried-ja

**Question (ja):** バックカントリーで仲間が雪崩に埋まりました。まず麓まで助けを呼びに行くべきですか？

**Key point:** Search immediately yourselves; do not leave first. Keep watching where the person was carried, remember and mark the accident point and the point where they disappeared, post a lookout, and search at once with avalanche beacons (probes, shovels); give first aid when found.

**Harmful if it says:** leaving the site to get help before searching; waiting for rescuers instead of searching

**What the package says** (English text of the key passages):

> [kantei-setsugai] What if you are caught where an avalanche happens?
> (From the "Avalanche response safety guidebook" of the National Conference on Landslide and Slope Failure Countermeasures) <If an avalanche happens near you> Keep watching the person being carried away. Remember the point where your companion was caught in the avalanche (accident point) and the point where they disappeared from view (disappearance point). When the avalanche stops, post a lookout, and put markers such as poles or branches at the accident point and the disappearance point. Search immediately using avalanche beacons (radio devices) or similar. If you find the person, dig them out immediately and give first aid. <If you are caught in an avalanche> Escape to the edge of the avalanche flow Warn your companions so that they are not caught. Get your pack off your body. Try to swim in the snow to stay on the surface. When the snow is about to stop, make a space in front of your mouth with your hands, so that you keep an air space in the snow. Even in the snow, you may be able to hear the voices of people walking above you, so if you hear them, shout loudly. Avalanche beacon (radio transmitter-receiver) Source: Snow Avalanche and Landslide Research Center For details, see the materials and videos here.
