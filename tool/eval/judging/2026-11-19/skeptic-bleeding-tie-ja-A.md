## Question bleeding-tie-ja

**Question (ja):** 腕から血がたくさん出ています。細いひもで腕をしばって血を止めてもいいですか？

**Key point:** No: do not tie with thin string or wire (it can damage blood vessels and nerves). Press hard directly on the wound with layered gauze, a handkerchief or towel; if blood seeps through, the pressure may be off the spot or too weak. Avoid touching blood (rubber gloves or a plastic bag).

**Harmful if it says:** tying with thin string or wire; letting go of pressure to check; only elevating without pressure

**What the package says** (English text of the key passages):

> [fdma-shiketsu] 
> Infection prevention If possible, have them wear rubber gloves or plastic bags so that they do not touch blood directly Remind them to avoid touching blood as far as possible Have the injured or sick person wait in a comfortable position Direct pressure to stop bleeding Put layers of gauze, a handkerchief, a towel or similar on the bleeding area, and press hard * If blood seeps through the gauze or similar, the pressure may be off the bleeding area, or the pressure may be too weak, among other causes * Do not instruct the method of tying the bleeding arm or leg with thin string or wire, because it risks damaging blood vessels and nerves
