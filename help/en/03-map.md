# The map

## What does the map show?

Evacuation sites for each kind of hazard and shelters for staying, expected tsunami areas, and the ways you can walk. Once a map area is downloaded, it works without internet.

## How do I find the nearest evacuation site?

Home shows the nearest site and how many minutes it is on foot. Tap it to see the way on the map. The right place depends on the hazard: a tsunami site is not always a flood site.

## Why is a shelter marked "unconfirmed" still suggested?

Reports from residents, and notices whose sender could not be checked, are shown with "unconfirmed". Anyone on the same Wi-Fi can send one, so the app does not let them change the suggested site, the way or the hazard. Notices from a base whose code you compared change them. Read an unconfirmed report as a hint and look for yourself on the way.

## It says "No map yet"

No map area is downloaded yet. Choose one for your area in "First steps", or in Settings → "Offline data" → "Downloads".

## Is the evacuation site information correct?

The sites come from the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan, which collects what each city registers. They can be out of date or missing; check with your city for the latest.
