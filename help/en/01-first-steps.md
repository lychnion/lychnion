# First steps

## What should I do first?

Follow "First steps" on Home: download the answer AI, add a map of where you live or are going, look at your nearest evacuation site on the map, and ask one question. When all are done, Home shows "Ready offline".

## Why do I have to download first?

Lychnion keeps the AI, the guides and the maps on this device, so it can answer without a signal. That needs one download first, on Wi-Fi: about 2.8 GB for the answer AI and about 310 MB for the search AI.

## I tapped "Later". Where do I continue?

Tap "Continue first steps" at the top of Home to show the checklist again.

## How do I learn where everything is?

Open "⋮" at the top right and choose "Guided tour". It shows the parts of Home and the tabs one by one.
