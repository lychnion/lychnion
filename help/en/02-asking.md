# Asking questions

## How do I ask?

Type your question in your own words in the field at the bottom of Home or the "Ask" tab, and send it. The "Ask" tab also has example questions for each installed package. You can ask in Japanese or English.

## Where do the answers come from?

Answers use only the guides in the knowledge packages on this device, mostly official pages from the national and local governments. Every answer shows its sources; tap one to read the original.

## What is the first line of an answer?

For dangers such as tsunamis, the answer starts with a fixed official safety line, not with text written by the AI. Follow that line first.

## Are there things it cannot answer?

It does not answer what its guides do not cover, and it says when they only cover part of a question. Lychnion does not replace official warnings or your city's instructions; always follow those first.

## Where are my past questions?

In the "Ask" tab. Swipe right to archive one, left to delete it. They are kept only on this device.
