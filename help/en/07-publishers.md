# Publishers and catalogs

## What is a publisher?

A person or group that makes guides and maps and signs them. Lychnion signs its own guides; cities and clubs can publish theirs too.

## How do I add a publisher, such as my city?

Open the link or QR code the publisher gives you; the app asks before it adds it. The list is in Settings → "Offline data" → "Publishers". Add only publishers you know. Removing one also deletes its guides and maps from this device.

## What does "Checked by Lychnion" mean?

Lychnion checked that the publisher's key belongs to it, for example on a city's official website, and signed a note saying so. The app shows the name Lychnion checked. A publisher you added that Lychnion has not checked shows "Added by you · not checked by Lychnion": it still works, Lychnion just does not know it. When Lychnion checks it later, the label changes by itself.

Answers show the same: under a passage from another publisher's guide, a line says whose guide it is and whether Lychnion checked that publisher. Answers from a base show the base's label.

The label never lets a publisher do more: only Lychnion's guides give the official safety line.

## Why does the app ask me to compare a code?

Anyone can make a link with any name. When a link's name looks like a city, a ministry or another public body, or like a publisher Lychnion checked, the app warns you and asks you to tick "I compared the code" first. Compare the key's code shown in the dialog with the one on the body's official website, or ask staff at the office or shelter. If they differ, do not add it.

A red "Not the key Lychnion checked" or "Lychnion took back its check" means the link is probably not from that publisher.

## What is a catalog?

A publisher's list of guides and maps. Add it in Settings → "Offline data" → "Catalogs" → "Follow a catalog", and its items appear in Downloads. Only items signed by Lychnion or by a publisher you added are shown.

## How do I publish my own guides?

On a computer, open "Publishers" → "Publish your own" and create a signing key; it stays on that computer. Phones that scan the QR code shown there trust your guides. Keep a copy of the key offline.
