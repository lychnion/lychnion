# Base

## What is a base?

A computer with Lychnion that helps the phones on the same Wi-Fi, for example in a shelter, a mountain hut or a school. It answers their questions with its own AI, shares current information such as shelter status, and lets them download guides and maps. No internet is needed.

## How does a phone connect to a base?

Scan the base's QR code with the normal camera. The "In the browser" code works without the app; the "With the app" code opens Lychnion and connects it. The phone and the computer must be on the same Wi-Fi.

## How do I make my computer a base?

In Settings → "Base", turn on "Use this computer as a base" and enter a name, for example "South Primary School shelter". Show the QR code to people; "Show large" fills the screen.

## Phones cannot connect

Guest and office Wi-Fi often blocks devices from reaching each other. The computer's own hotspot or a Wi-Fi router the shelter controls works best.
