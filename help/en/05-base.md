# Base

## What is a base?

A computer with Lychnion that helps the phones on the same Wi-Fi, for example in a shelter, a mountain hut or a school. It answers their questions with its own AI, shares current information such as shelter status, and lets them download guides and maps. No internet is needed.

## How does a phone connect to a base?

Scan the base's QR code with the normal camera. The "In the browser" code works without the app: its page answers questions, but it is not checked, and anyone on the same Wi-Fi could change it, so it shows no shelter status or warnings (check those on the base's screen and with the staff); the "With the app" code opens Lychnion and connects it. The phone and the computer must be on the same Wi-Fi.

## Why compare a code?

To the phone, a link from a web page or a message looks just like the base's QR code. So before it connects, Lychnion shows the base's code, and the base's screen shows its code under the QR code. Tick the box only if the two codes are the same: then the base's notices, such as shelter status, show as official. Until then they show as unconfirmed.

## Which bases do I trust?

Settings → "Base" lists the bases whose codes you compared, under "Trusted bases". "Stop trusting" makes a base's notices unconfirmed again. To compare the code of the connected base later, tap the base in Settings → "Base".

## How do I make my computer a base?

In Settings → "Base", turn on "Use this computer as a base" and enter a name, for example "South Primary School shelter". Show the QR code to people; "Show large" fills the screen.

## Phones cannot connect

Guest and office Wi-Fi often blocks devices from reaching each other. The computer's own hotspot or a Wi-Fi router the shelter controls works best.
