# When something does not work

## I cannot ask a question

The answer AI may not be downloaded yet. Get it in "First steps" on Home, or in Settings → "Offline data" → "Downloads". While it loads, questions are taken and answered as soon as it is ready.

## Answers take a long time

On older phones one answer can take close to a minute. A base nearby answers much faster.

## A download stopped

It continues where it stopped once you are back on Wi-Fi. If the device is full, delete models or maps you don't use.

## I cannot find the base or the other phone

Check that both are on the same Wi-Fi. Guest Wi-Fi often blocks devices from reaching each other.
