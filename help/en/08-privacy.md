# Privacy

## Are my questions or my position sent anywhere?

Your position never leaves this device, and there is no account. Questions and answers stay on this device too, with one exception: when you are connected to a base, your questions go to that base so it can answer. A base is always a computer on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot; the app never sends questions to the internet. Others on that Wi-Fi may be able to read them, so do not ask a base anything private.

## When does the app use the internet?

Only to download models, guides and maps, and to check for new versions. When connected to a base, it talks only to that base on the same Wi-Fi.
