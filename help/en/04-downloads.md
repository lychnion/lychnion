# Downloads

## What can I download?

AI models for answers and search, knowledge packages (disaster preparedness, hiking, first aid and more) and map areas. The list is in Settings → "Offline data" → "Downloads".

## What are the place names under the titles?

They show which area a package or map is for. Items for your region are listed first. Your region is set in Settings → "Region", or worked out from the device's position.

## What does "Signed" mean?

The publisher checked and signed the item. Lychnion checks the signature and the files before it uses them, so files from another phone or a base are safe to use too.

## Does it use mobile data?

At first it downloads only on Wi-Fi. In Downloads, the settings button at the top right offers "Wi-Fi only", "Ask first" and "Always". A base nearby offers its Wi-Fi for downloads too.

## How do I get new versions?

A mark on the menu and in Downloads shows when a new version is available. Updates can be "Automatic", "Notify" or "Off"; "Automatic" downloads them only on Wi-Fi.

## What does "a newer version exists, but it is not available here yet" mean?

Lychnion signs a short note saying which version of each of its items is the newest (other publishers may later do the same). When that note names a newer version than the one your USB stick, base or download source offers, the app tells you. The old version keeps working. Get the new one when you can: from the internet, another base, or a newer stick.

Downloads also shows when you last got news from Lychnion. After 30 days without news, it reminds you gently that the data may be out of date.
