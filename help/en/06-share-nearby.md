# Sharing with people nearby

## Can I give guides and maps to another phone without internet?

Yes. Open "Share with people nearby" from "⋮" at the top right, or from the share button in Downloads. While that screen is open, the other phone can take your downloaded guides, maps and models.

## What does the other phone do?

Scan the QR code with the camera. Lychnion opens and shows what it can receive in Downloads; pick only what you need. Everything is checked against its signature before it is saved.

## What is needed?

Both phones on the same Wi-Fi, or one phone's hotspot. Phones don't answer each other's questions (too slow).
